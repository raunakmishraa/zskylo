## Public-facing content

Fees, milestones, deposits, cancellation terms and any refund eligibility for a project will be described in the relevant written proposal or service agreement before work begins.

If you have a question about a payment or an agreed project, contact [hello@zskylo.com](mailto:hello@zskylo.com) and include the relevant project or invoice reference. Requests will be reviewed against the written agreement.

## Internal review notes

Do not invent a universal refund window or guarantee. Confirm how deposits, work already completed, third-party costs, cancellations and disputes are handled, then have the policy reviewed for applicable Nepalese law and any client-market requirements.
